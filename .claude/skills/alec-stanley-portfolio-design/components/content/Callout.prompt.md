Pulls a result or caveat out of a project write-up. Left rule only — never a full border, never a rounded left-accent card.

```jsx
<Callout tone="accent" title="Result">Predicted chamber pressure within 4% of measured across 6 static fires.</Callout>
<Callout tone="warn">Model assumes steady-state combustion; transients are out of scope.</Callout>
```

At most two per page.
