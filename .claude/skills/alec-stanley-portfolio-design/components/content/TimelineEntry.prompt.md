A row in the experience / education timeline. Stack them directly; the top hairline supplies the separator.

```jsx
<TimelineEntry current period="2023 — NOW" title="Bachelor of Engineering (Hons) / Science"
  org="Monash University" tags={["Aerospace","Astrophysics"]}
  description="Fourth year of a five-year double degree." />
```

Periods are uppercase mono with an em dash. Newest first.
